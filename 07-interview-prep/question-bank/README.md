## How to drill (the method that works)

1. **Out loud, timed, standing.** Reading questions ≠ prep. 25 minutes for design/strategy; 10 for technical explains; 3 for behaviorals.
2. **Record → listen → once more.** The second take after hearing yourself is where improvement happens.
3. **Rotate categories daily.** Loops interleave them; so should you.
4. **After each answer, self-score 3 things:** Did I structure first? Did I hit the AI beats (why-AI, failure design, evals)? Did I land a recommendation?
5. **Escalate to live mocks** in week 2+: peers, or platforms (Exponent, PM Exercises, IGotAnOffer coaching; [verified list](../README.md)).
