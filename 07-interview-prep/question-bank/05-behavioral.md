# 🗣️ Question Bank — Behavioral, Leadership & Mission Fit

> Prep = the [8-story portfolio](../answer-frameworks.md) in STAR-AI format, quantified, 90 seconds each. Frontier labs add a mission/values layer that cannot be faked well — build real opinions before you interview. 🔵 = reported/sourced · ⚪ = practice.

---

## Tier 1 — Reported 🔵

1. "Walk me through the most difficult product launch you've led. What made it hard?" *(OpenAI recruiter screen)*
2. "If you were already an OpenAI PM on this team, what would you do and why?" *(OpenAI hiring manager — do the homework: know the team's product surface, have a 90-day PoV)*
3. "Tell me about a time you cut scope to ship faster." *(OpenAI)*
4. "Can you give an example of a trade-off between user experience and technical feasibility?" *(via PM Accelerator's OpenAI list)*
5. "Why Anthropic, and what draws you to AI safety?" *(Anthropic recruiter — specificity test: name the writings/ideas that actually moved you)*
6. "What are some unusual beliefs you hold, and how have you defended them?" *(Anthropic culture round — tests independent thinking + intellectual courage; a safe, rehearsed-sounding answer IS a failing answer)*
7. "What concerns do you have with Anthropic's direction?" *(Anthropic — genuine, informed disagreement scores; flattery doesn't)*
8. "Tell me about a time you did something that conflicted with your values." *(Anthropic)*
9. "Tell me about a time you received harsh or critical feedback." *(Meta leadership & drive)*
10. "How would you convince engineers to build a feature?" *(Perplexity — evidence + shared metrics + prototype > authority)*
11. "Tell me about an AI product from your resume. What did you like and dislike about it?" *(Microsoft AI — have the balanced critique ready)*
12. "What experience do you have building AI features?" *(Perplexity — if the honest answer is thin, [build the portfolio](../../04-build-with-ai/projects.md) before you apply)*
13. "Tell me about a time you shipped an AI feature that backfired." *(cross-company via [IGotAnOffer](https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/ai-product-manager-interview) — the Learning beat is the whole answer)*
14. "Describe a situation where you had to explain AI limitations to excited stakeholders." *(cross-company via IGotAnOffer)*

## Tier 2 — AI-era behavioral practice ⚪

15. Tell me about a time you made a call with fundamentally incomplete information. How did you bound the risk?
16. Describe pushing back on leadership's pet AI idea. What evidence did you bring, and what happened?
17. Tell me about balancing shipping speed against a quality/safety concern. Where did you draw the line and why?
18. Describe learning a technical domain fast because your role demanded it. Show the method, not just the outcome.
19. Tell me about a disagreement with a data scientist/ML engineer where you were wrong. *(the being-wrong stories are the senior ones)*
20. How do you personally use AI in your PM work today — and where have you decided NOT to use it? *(the second half reveals judgment; have a real verification-cost answer — [build-with-ai §4](../../04-build-with-ai/README.md))*
21. Tell me about earning trust with a skeptical engineering team.
22. Describe a time you killed a project you'd championed. What was the tell?
23. A teammate ships a prompt change that causes a public embarrassment. You own the feature. Walk me through your first 48 hours. *(incident response + blameless posture + eval-gap fix — [responsible-ai §8](../../03-product-craft/responsible-ai.md))*
24. Where do you think AI will genuinely change product management in 5 years, and what are you doing about it now? *(vision + agency check)*

## Tier 3 — Mission & judgment (frontier-lab layer) ⚪

25. What's an AI capability you think should NOT be built or shipped, even if profitable? Defend it.
26. Describe a time short-term metrics and long-term user wellbeing conflicted. What did you choose?
27. What do you believe about AI progress that most people around you don't? *(the "unusual beliefs" pattern generalized — prepare 2 genuine ones with reasoning)*
28. If you joined and discovered the team's flagship feature had a serious safety gap two weeks before a committed launch, what would you actually do — step by step?
29. Steelman the position opposite yours on open-weight frontier models. Then say where you land and why.

## Calibration notes

- **Anthropic's culture round is reported by candidates as the decisive one** — treat it with the same prep weight as product sense. Read their published work (Constitutional AI, responsible-scaling thinking, model cards); form real views; bring at least one respectful disagreement.
- **Amazon**: map every story to named Leadership Principles *before* the loop; interviewers literally score against them, and the Bar Raiser will probe your weakest story's numbers.
- **OpenAI**: expect "why this team" specificity plus velocity-vs-safety tension somewhere in the loop; concrete mechanisms (gates, evals, tiered rollouts) beat philosophical positioning.
- **The universal tell of a strong candidate**: quantified results, a genuine failure story told without spin, and an unprompted "what I'd do differently now."
