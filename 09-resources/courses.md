## Technical depth & executive tracks

- **Stanford AI Professional Program** (online.stanford.edu) — graduate-lecture depth, certificate after 3 courses. For PMs who want real technical weight.
- **fast.ai Practical Deep Learning** (course.fast.ai) — free; the legendary hands-on DL course if you code a little.
- **AWS Skill Builder** (skillbuilder.aws) — 600+ free courses incl. "Generative AI with LLMs" (with DeepLearning.AI).
- **MIT Sloan: AI — Implications for Business Strategy** (~$3,200–3,850, 6 wks) and **Kellogg: AI-Driven Product Strategy** (8 wks) — exec-ed credentials; buy for network + employer-funded budgets, not for content you can't get cheaper above.
