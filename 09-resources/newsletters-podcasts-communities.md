# 📬 Newsletters, Podcasts, YouTube & Communities

> The living layer of AI PM knowledge, where the field updates weekly. All verified active with correct URLs, **July 2026**. Curate ruthlessly: pick ~4 subscriptions and actually read them; 15 unread newsletters is a mood, not a strategy.

## Newsletters: the recommended stack by role

**The PM core (pick 2):**
- **[Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/)**: the #1 product career/craft newsletter; its AI PM guides (evals, prototyping, Meta's AI interview round) are repeatedly cited in this repo. Free tier + paid (~$150-200/yr; paid unlocks the 30K-member Slack).
- **[Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/)** (Aakash Gupta): the most AI-PM-focused mainstream PM newsletter; deep interview-prep content (free + paid tiers).
- **[Marily's AI Product Academy](https://marily.substack.com/)** (Marily Nika): AI-PM-specific tools/workflows/jobs from the field's best-known instructor.
- **[Behind the Craft](https://creatoreconomy.so/)** (Peter Yang): practical AI tutorials for product people.

**The AI-literacy layer (pick 1-2):**
- **[One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/)** (Ethan Mollick): research-grounded, hype-resistant essays on using frontier AI; the best signal-per-word in the category.
- **[The Neuron](https://www.theneurondaily.com/)**: most beginner-friendly daily digest · **[The Rundown AI](https://www.therundown.ai/)**: biggest daily (2M+ readers) · **[TLDR AI](https://tldr.tech/ai)**: densest triage · **[Ben's Bites](https://www.bensbites.com/)**: builder-oriented daily. *(Pick ONE daily; they overlap heavily.)*

**The depth layer (when fluent):**
- **[Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/)**: where AI engineering/product discourse actually happens; their "AI Engineer" framing named the adjacent role.
- **[Interconnects](https://www.interconnects.ai/)** (Nathan Lambert): the best writing on how models are actually trained/post-trained.
- **[Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/)** (Jack Clark, Anthropic co-founder): research + policy, running since 2016.
- **[Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/)** (Ben Thompson): the strategy lens on AI platform wars; pairs with [our strategy section](../03-product-craft/ai-product-strategy.md).

## YouTube: the technical intuition shortcut

- **[3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown)**, the neural-networks series: the most beautiful visual explanation of how NNs/transformers work. Watch before any interview with a technical round.
- **[Andrej Karpathy](https://www.youtube.com/andrejkarpathy)**. "Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT" (~3.5 hrs) and "Intro to Large Language Models" are the single best explanations of the [three-stage pipeline](../01-foundations/llm-fundamentals.md) in existence: general-audience, from a founding OpenAI researcher.
- **[StatQuest](https://www.youtube.com/c/joshstarmer)** (Josh Starmer): bite-sized ML/stats concepts, zero intimidation.
- **[Exponent](https://www.youtube.com/@tryexponent)**: mock PM interviews on camera, including AI PM questions. Watch 2-3 before your first mock to calibrate pacing.

## The consumption system (so this stays useful)

**Daily (10 min):** one daily digest. **Weekly (1 hr):** Lenny's/Aakash deep-dive + one podcast on commute. **Monthly (2 hrs):** one Karpathy/3B1B-depth video or one long technical post, converted into notes → [glossary](../01-foundations/glossary.md) self-test. **Always:** when a story matters (model launch, big failure, acquisition), read the primary source. You're training the [verification habit](../06-case-studies/README.md) that interviews and the job both reward.
