## Negotiation notes specific to AI PM

1. **Level is the negotiation.** AI PM roles frequently get down-leveled in screening (OpenAI is explicit about candid leveling talks). A level = $100K+ at frontier labs; argue level with scope evidence (org size influenced, revenue owned, ambiguity handled), not enthusiasm.
2. **Posted ranges are anchors, not caps.** Top-of-range needs a competing signal; the strongest one is another offer, and AI PM loops run fast enough (Anthropic ~19 days average) to parallelize.
3. **Equity literacy matters more here than anywhere**: frontier-lab TC is mostly equity whose valuation moved multiple times in 2024-26. Ask: strike/valuation basis, refresh policy, liquidity history (tender offers), and vesting cliffs. A $700K TC at a paper valuation is not a $700K salary.
4. **Domain premium is real**: regulated-domain AI PMs (health, fin) command scarcity pricing at the intersection ([domains](../05-domains/README.md)).
5. **India negotiation**: the P90 (₹80 LPA) exists, and it concentrates at AI-natives and global-cap companies hiring in India; the title-arbitrage move is applying to global companies' India orgs rather than domestic incumbents.
